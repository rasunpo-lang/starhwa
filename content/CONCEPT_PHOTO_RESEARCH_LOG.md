# Concept Photo Research Log — ATEEZ Official Platform (KQ) + Alternate Sources

Tracks the sweep through ATEEZ's Korean/Japanese album eras for Seonghwa's official concept photos/images, per the request to go through `ateez.kqent.com` and identify his individual concept photos, using alternate official sources where the KQ site doesn't cover a period. Not part of the published archive (lives outside `content/entries/`, never read by the content loader).

## Methodology

1. **ATEEZ Official Platform (`ateez.kqent.com`)** — the site was not login-gated for browsing; a public tag feed at `ateez.kqent.com/tag/성화` (and its underlying API, `/svc/home/api/v1/feeds/tagged?tag=성화`) lists every piece of content KQ has tagged with Seonghwa's name, with exact publish timestamps. This feed is **exhaustive but only goes back to May 2023** (its earliest item is the "OPERATION" preview poster for THE WORLD EP.2) — the platform itself appears to have launched with THE WORLD EP.2 and has no retroactive content for TREASURE, ZERO: FEVER, or THE WORLD EP.1. Individual "Members only" story/diary posts on the platform are gated behind login and were skipped since they aren't concept photos.
2. **Alternate official source (ATEEZ's official X account)** — used for every era before THE WORLD EP.2, per instructions. Each candidate was opened and read directly (not taken from a search snippet) before being counted, consistent with this archive's existing verification standard.
3. Cross-tag verification: for KQ items without an explicit member name in the title (e.g. GOLDEN HOUR: Part.1's "Concept Photo 1–4"), Seonghwa-exclusivity was confirmed by checking the same content ID did **not** appear in another member's (Hongjoong's) tag feed.
