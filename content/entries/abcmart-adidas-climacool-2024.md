ATEEZ (all eight members, including Seonghwa) fronted a campaign for adidas's "CLIMACOOL" sneaker line, sold through Japanese retailer ABC-MART, under the theme "#風を変えよう" ("Change the Wind"). ABC-MART's own press release, dated April 23, 2024, confirms the group's involvement with a 30-second campaign movie and interview quotes from Hongjoong, Yeosang, Mingi, Yunho, and Wooyoung; visuals and movie released April 25, 2024. No source directly from ATEEZ's own official platform was found confirming this campaign, so it is marked unverified.
