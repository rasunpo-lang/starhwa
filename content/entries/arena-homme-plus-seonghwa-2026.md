Seonghwa fronted Arena Homme Plus's August 2026 digital cover and interview, reported July 27, 2026. He discusses using photo shoots to develop an identity separate from his idol work: "화보를 찍을 때마다 제 만족도가 올라가는 느낌이에요... 팀으로 보여주는 모습이 있고, 또 개인으로 보여줄 수 있는 모습이 있잖아요" ("My satisfaction rises every time I shoot a pictorial... there's a side I show as a team, and a side I can show as an individual"), and on creative reinvention with each comeback: "에이티즈 음악 안에서 어울리는 저를 찾아야 한다는 생각이 있죠... 매번 벽에 부딪히는 느낌도 들어요. 하지만 아직까지는 계속 도전하는 게 맞다고 생각해요" ("I have to find the version of myself that fits ATEEZ's music... I feel like I hit a wall every time, but I still think continuing to challenge myself is the right approach"). Directly confirmed by Newsen's own reported interview text.

Seonghwa's Words — English Translation

This is a magazine feature written around his quotes rather than a printed Q&A transcript, so the topics below are drawn from the article's framing rather than quoted verbatim from an interviewer.

On the thrill of shooting pictorials: "Every time I shoot a pictorial, I feel like my sense of satisfaction goes up. I'm active as an idol right now, but I think of these pictorial shoots as time where I build my own color. There's a side of myself I show as part of the team, and there's a side I can show as an individual. That's why this kind of time is precious to me."

On rebuilding himself with every ATEEZ comeback: "I have this idea that I need to find the version of myself that fits within ATEEZ's music. Of course, it's not easy — I feel like I hit a wall every single time. It'd be comfortable to just do what suits me well. But I still think, for now, that continuing to challenge myself is the right thing to do. In that sense, this activity too was time where I built one more character that's uniquely mine."

Translated directly from the original Korean text of the Newsen (via Daum) article linked below.
