Seonghwa's individual section from Arena Homme Plus's 2020 group feature on ATEEZ.

<!-- full-text -->

Published October 3, 2020, this group feature profiled all eight ATEEZ members individually through short topic-based reflections. In his section, Seonghwa discusses the meaning behind his name and stage nickname ("become a star"), his effort-driven approach to the group, and the moment a dance-club performance led to him being scouted as a trainee.

He also reflects on getting through hard times using a lyric from "Sunrise," a moment during "Wonderland" when dancing all-out felt more authentic than trying to look polished, and how meeting ATINY after debut helped him move past the self-doubt of his trainee years. He closes by naming the members he turns to with different kinds of worries, and admitting his biggest fear during the pandemic was performing to an empty venue for so long.
