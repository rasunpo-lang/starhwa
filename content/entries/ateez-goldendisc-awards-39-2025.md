ATEEZ's Album Division Bonsang at the 39th Golden Disc Awards.

The 39th Golden Disc Awards was held January 4–5, 2025, at Mizuho PayPay Dome in Fukuoka. ATEEZ received an Album Bonsang for THE WORLD EP.FIN : WILL on the second day of the ceremony.
