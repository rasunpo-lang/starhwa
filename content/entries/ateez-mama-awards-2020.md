ATEEZ's performance and two awards at the 2020 Mnet Asian Music Awards: Discovery of the Year and Worldwide Fans' Choice.

<!-- full-text -->

The 2020 MAMA was held on December 6, 2020, in South Korea. ATEEZ performed at the ceremony, taking the stage with "Dona Eis Requiem," a medley of "INCEPTION" and "Answer," and were named in the Worldwide Fans' Choice Top 10 and given the Discovery of the Year award, their second MAMA recognition after the 2019 Worldwide Fans' Choice.
