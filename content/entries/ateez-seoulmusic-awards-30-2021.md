ATEEZ's Main Award (Bonsang) at the 30th Seoul Music Awards.

<!-- full-text -->

The 30th Seoul Music Awards took place on January 31, 2021, without an on-site audience because of the pandemic. ATEEZ was among the Bonsang (Main Award) recipients, nominated for ZERO : FEVER Part.1, and also performed during the broadcast.
