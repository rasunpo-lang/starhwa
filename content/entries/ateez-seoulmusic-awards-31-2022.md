ATEEZ's Main Award (Bonsang) at the 31st Seoul Music Awards.

<!-- full-text -->

The 31st Seoul Music Awards was held on January 23, 2022, at Gocheok Sky Dome. ATEEZ was one of thirteen acts honored with a Bonsang, their second in a row at the ceremony.

Open News Agency reported that ATEEZ was in the United States for the THE FELLOWSHIP : BEGINNING OF THE END arena tour and so accepted the award through a thank-you video rather than in person. In it they said the love and support they received through 2021 let them keep running without tiring, called the award an honor, and promised better stages and music in return. The report recapped the group's 2021: the ZERO : FEVER Part.2 and Part.3 mini-albums and the ZERO : FEVER EPILOGUE repackage, a first Billboard 200 entry at No. 42, and roughly 1.86 million cumulative album sales for the year. It added that their third U.S. tour show was set for the following day at the Prudential Center in Newark.
