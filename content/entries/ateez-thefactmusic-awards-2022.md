ATEEZ's Artist of the Year (Bonsang) and Best Performer awards at the 2022 The Fact Music Awards.

The 2022 The Fact Music Awards was held on October 8, 2022, at the KSPO Dome in Seoul. ATEEZ received Artist of the Year (Bonsang) and shared the Best Performer award with NCT DREAM.
