Seonghwa's episode of BS-TBS's solo-documentary series "ATEEZ 8FACE" (Ep.7).

<!-- full-text -->

Japanese satellite broadcaster BS-TBS's "ATEEZ 8FACE" — a world-first solo-documentary format for the group, one episode per member — devoted its seventh episode entirely to Seonghwa: a pre-debut retrospective interview (Side-A) paired with a personal-day segment following him to Legoland, tied to his own well-known Lego-collecting hobby (Side-B). Broadcast February 18, 2025, with TVer streaming from the following day.
