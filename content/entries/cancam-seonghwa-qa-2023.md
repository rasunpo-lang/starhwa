A Q&A interview with Seonghwa published by CanCam to mark ATEEZ's Japan 2nd single "Limitless" and their "ANCHOR IN JAPAN" encore concerts. Published March 24, 2023.

Seonghwa's Words — Translated Highlights

Asked about the highlight of "Limitless," Seonghwa pointed to the moment before the bridge where the instrumentation drops out entirely and only San's voice remains, calling it the song's real standout moment. Asked what he could eat endlessly, he named abura soba as his favorite food. Given infinite time, he said he'd want to meet more ATINY and build precious memories together. Asked about a moment he felt he'd surpassed his own limits, he described the strength that wells up hearing ATINY's cheers and seeing their eyes at concerts, saying it feels like overcoming his limits together with them. Asked for something he tells himself in hard moments, he described a personal mantra — paraphrased here rather than quoted directly — about striving to be the best for the people who need him, and wanting to keep moving toward a high point rather than a stopping point.

On performing demanding choreography while singing live, he said he sometimes exceeds his own limits without realizing it, so he focuses on conditioning himself carefully before every show to perform safely. Reflecting on their December Japan concert (roughly 30,000 attendees, with a first-time trolley staging effect), he called it a truly special experience, since he was able to greet even fans seated far from the stage. Asked to name a standout member for 2022 and a most-anticipated member for 2023, he said every member deserved the "hardest-working" title equally, and picked himself as most anticipated, saying he wants to expect more of himself and contribute more to the group. He mentioned getting into "Animal Crossing" and finding a character named Yukimi especially cute. Asked what Korean food to recommend to visiting Japanese ATINY, he suggested samgyeopsal, cooked tableside with a spread of side dishes. He closed by thanking ATINY for their continued support and asking them to keep expecting good things from the group.
