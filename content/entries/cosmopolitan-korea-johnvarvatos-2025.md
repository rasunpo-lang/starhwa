A solo pictorial and written interview for Cosmopolitan Korea's May 2025 issue, pairing Seonghwa with premium fragrance house John Varvatos, published April 21, 2025. In the post-shoot interview he says he tried on every John Varvatos scent beforehand and that "Artisan" and "Artisan Black" matched his taste exactly, surprising him enough that he photographed the bottles to repurchase later, and notes he's very sensitive to scent and picky about fragrance generally. He also discusses a recent birthday gift (a bedside lamp he'd admired in a department store, gifted by the CEO and members after he mentioned it), previews ATEEZ's July 2025 world tour opening at Inspire Arena Incheon, and reflects on happiness: "행복은 감사한 마음과 일맥상통하는 것 같아요... 그래서 저는 지금 행복해요" ("I think happiness is tied to gratitude... so I'm happy right now"), naming Marimond (연) as his favorite flower for its flower language, "happiness that will surely come."

Seonghwa's Words — English Translation

This is a magazine feature written around his quotes rather than a printed Q&A transcript, so questions below are reconstructed from the article's topic framing rather than quoted verbatim from the reporter.

On the John Varvatos fragrances: "Ahead of the shoot, I tried on every John Varvatos fragrance. 'Artisan' and 'Artisan Black' matched my taste the most. I was so surprised — surprised enough that I even took photos of them. Once I finish them, I'm planning to buy more. I'm very sensitive to scent, so I'm usually picky about choosing fragrances, but these two hit my taste exactly."

On his recent birthday: "There was a lamp I happened to see at a department store before — it was pretty expensive to buy for myself. I thought it'd be nice if someone gifted it to me for my birthday. (laughs) I mentioned it to the CEO and the members, and they gave it to me right on my birthday." He added that he plans to set it up by his bedside, then continued on his current activities: "Since there haven't been that many visible schedules lately, ATINY [ATEEZ's fandom name] is probably curious about how I've been. I've been working out hard — it's become a habit now — and filming all kinds of content too. I'm diligently preparing to come meet you all, so please wait just a little longer!"

On the upcoming world tour: "We're ATEEZ. (laughs) If you come see this show too, I promise you won't regret that choice. The reason I can say that even though the show isn't fully planned out yet is because I trust our members, our company, and the staff who build the stage together with us. And because we always give our best on stage, I'm confident."

On whether he's happy right now: "These days, the emotion I feel most often is happiness. In the past, happiness felt really far away to me. I used to think a lot about things like, 'will I ever be happy? Is this path right? Is happiness even something I'm allowed?' But looking back on it now, happiness was definitely there beside me even then. I think I just didn't know how to accept it. I didn't have enough room to breathe, and I wasn't mature enough." He continued: "I think happiness is tied to a grateful heart. So I'm happy right now. I hope the people around me, and all of ATINY, are happy too."

Note: the line about Marimond being his favorite flower and its meaning is the reporter's own description, not a direct Seonghwa quote, so it is not presented as translated speech above. Translated directly from the original Korean text of the Cosmopolitan Korea article linked below.
