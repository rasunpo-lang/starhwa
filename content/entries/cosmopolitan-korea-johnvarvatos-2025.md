A solo Cosmopolitan Korea pictorial and interview with Seonghwa for the May 2025 issue, featuring John Varvatos fragrances.

<!-- full-text -->

Seonghwa talks about his fragrance preferences, a birthday gift he received from the company and members, ATEEZ's upcoming world tour, and his thoughts on happiness and gratitude. The interview was published on April 21, 2025, following the May 2025 issue shoot.

He describes John Varvatos Artisan and Artisan Black as the fragrances that best matched his taste, and shares that he photographed the bottles so he could buy them again later. He also talks about receiving a bedside lamp he had admired as a birthday gift and mentions working out and preparing new content while waiting to meet ATINY again.

Looking ahead to ATEEZ's July 2025 world tour, he expresses confidence in the members, company and staff behind the show. He also reflects on how his understanding of happiness has changed, saying that he now connects happiness with gratitude and feels happy in the present.

Cosmopolitan Korea also describes Marimond (연) as his favorite flower and notes its flower meaning, "happiness that will surely come." This description comes from the article rather than from Seonghwa's own words.
