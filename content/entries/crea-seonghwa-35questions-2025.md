In CREA's card-based "35 Questions" series, marking the magazine's 35th anniversary, Seonghwa and Jongho each drew cards from CREA's full deck of 35 possible questions and answered them on camera — this episode's article and companion video cover four cards in total, not the full 35.

<!-- full-text -->

Picking cards one at a time from CREA's deck, Seonghwa went first. Asked his karaoke go-to, he named SG Wannabe's "Family Photo," a song he associates with singing together with his parents, since it's one they both like.

Jongho took the next card: asked about his go-to bag brand, he named Celine, which he said became an everyday favorite almost by accident.

Seonghwa then drew a card asking about the first CD he ever bought himself. He recalled a home collection full of albums and identified IU's single "Good Day" as his first personal purchase, alongside other albums by BIGBANG, 2NE1, and EXO he grew up with at home; when Jongho pressed him on which one came first, Seonghwa confirmed it was the IU album.

The last card asked about their oldest childhood memory. Seonghwa turned the question to Jongho first, asking if he remembered anything from infancy; Jongho said he couldn't recall anything before around age five, then told a story about a minor traffic accident during kindergarten — he came away physically unhurt, but was baffled that his teacher was so worried about him, since he didn't yet understand the concept of "worry" at that age. More than gratitude, he mainly remembered feeling embarrassed at being fussed over in front of everyone and just wanting to go home.

Seonghwa closed the segment by thanking viewers and hoping they'd enjoyed it, before both members signed off together.
