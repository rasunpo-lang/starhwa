In CREA's card-based "35 Questions" series, marking the magazine's 35th anniversary, Seonghwa answered a set of personal questions alongside Jongho.

Seonghwa's Answers — Translation

Asked his karaoke go-to, he named SG Wannabe's "Family Photo," a song he associates with singing together with his parents, since it's one they both like. Asked about the first CD he ever bought himself, he recalled a home collection full of albums and identified IU's single "Good Day" as his first personal purchase, alongside other albums by BIGBANG, 2NE1, and EXO he grew up with at home.
