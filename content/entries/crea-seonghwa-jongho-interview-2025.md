CREA's paired Seonghwa & Jongho interview on what they admire in each other.

<!-- full-text -->

In a paired interview for CREA's Spring 2025 Korea feature, Seonghwa and Jongho — the group's oldest and youngest members, only two years apart — discussed what they admire in each other and where their tastes diverge.

Seonghwa × Jongho Interview — Translation

Jongho described Seonghwa as someone whose warmth, protective older-brother quality, and self-discipline run deeper than his looks, pointing to a brief expression Seonghwa makes near the end of the "Ice On My Teeth" music video as a moment that instantly captures attention. Seonghwa, in turn, praised Jongho's energy, room-reading instincts, and role as a mediator and mood-maker among the members, and singled out Jongho's vocal ad-lib in the third verse of "Enough" as the emotional high point of that song. Both agreed their shared love of emotionally expressive music is what they have most in common.

Asked about their differences, the two named their shoe size as an easy example, and Seonghwa added that their styles diverge more broadly — he prefers flashier, more elaborate fashion, while Jongho favors simple, clean looks, and their travel habits differ the same way, with Jongho more spontaneous and Seonghwa a meticulous planner. Seonghwa said he still thinks the two of them would enjoy traveling together.
