CUT Japan's May 2024 cover excerpt: a roundtable with Hongjoong, Seonghwa, Yunho, and Mingi.

<!-- full-text -->

CUT's May 2024 issue features ATEEZ on the cover with a 24-page feature covering two four-member roundtable interviews based on the members' unit and solo tracks from THE WORLD EP.FIN : WILL. Before the issue was released, CUT's editorial blog shared a short excerpt from each member of the Hongjoong, Seonghwa, Yunho and Mingi group.

In Seonghwa's excerpt, he talks about wanting to become a good person before becoming a good singer, and about using that mindset to have a positive influence on ATINY. He also says he hopes to become an adult who can convey something meaningful to others through his music.

The full interview appears in the print issue. The excerpt was published by rockinon.com, with editor Abe Fumika credited.
