Seonghwa's Words — Translated Highlights, following the feature's own section format

Profile Introduction: "Despite being the oldest member, he's known for his generous, warm-hearted personality — taking his younger members' affectionate teasing in stride."

On ATEEZ's Appeal: "True to the name 'A to Z,' we're a complete group. We have everything — performance, dance, visuals, fashion, and kindness!"

Himself, in Three Hashtags: "#ATEEZ #UltimateHandsome #MemberSEONGHWA"

His ATINY Pride: "I want to pour a lot of love into them — they're something precious to me, something I'm proud of."

His Perfect Day: "Hmm... that's a tough question. I think it's different for everyone, but for me, a perfect day is one where I can feel calm and be able to accept myself."

1,000 Yen at a Japanese Convenience Store: "First, barley tea, pudding, ice cream, and snacks! There are a lot of things I really want to eat, but I'd like to buy some bread too."

A Member Who Moved Him Recently: "During the European tour, Wooyoung gave me a can of tuna. He'd brought it from Korea for his own diet management, so it was really precious — but he gave it to me anyway when we were eating together, and it really moved me."

His Message to the Members for the New Year: "Health matters most, so please don't get hurt. As long as I'm with the members, nothing scares me — I'm proud of them, and though it's a little embarrassing to say, I love them."
