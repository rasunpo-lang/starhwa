GQ's August 2024 group feature and pictorial on ATEEZ, written by Michelle Hyun Kim with photography by Eric Ray Davidson, touching on the group's Coachella performance and genre-spanning sound. For the shoot, Seonghwa wore a Needles jacket, R13 t-shirt, ERL shorts and sneakers, Falke socks, an Amiri hat, his own ring, and a Roberto Cavalli necklace. The full article text sits behind GQ's own site; the quote below is drawn from GQ's own published excerpt.

Seonghwa on the creative freedom the group found training in the US: "While we were training in the US, we learned about the idea of not really having a set answer. Through that, our dance, our creativity, our genre was able to widen."
