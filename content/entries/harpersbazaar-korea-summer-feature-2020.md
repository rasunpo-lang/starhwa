Harper's Bazaar Korea's ATEEZ summer group feature and interview.

<!-- full-text -->

A group pictorial and written interview with all eight members of ATEEZ for Harper's Bazaar Korea, published August 1, 2020, touching on the group's nicknames, their growth during a pandemic-forced hiatus, and an upcoming release.

Seonghwa's Words — English Translation

This is a joint group interview; only Seonghwa's own answers are translated below, in the order they appear in the original article.

Q: If there's something you've wanted to say to the members you're stuck with 24 hours a day, but have been too shy to say — here's your chance.
A: "I got injured a little while ago. Changing the choreography formation because of me must have been a physical burden, but the members told me not to worry about it and looked after me first. I want to take this chance to say thank you."

Q: Let's try defining ATEEZ using your own lyrics. Which line fits?
A: "From the song 'Say My Name' — 'My name is A to the Z.'"

Translated directly from the original Korean text of the Harper's Bazaar Korea article.
