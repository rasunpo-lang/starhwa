Seonghwa was announced as part of the cast of KBS's idol-industry variety drama "Imitation," playing Seyoung, leader of the fictional boy group Sparkling — alongside ATEEZ members Yunho, Jongho, and San. The casting was reported October 16, 2020; the drama itself aired in 2021. Character-name spelling varies slightly across secondary sources (Seyoung/Se Young), and no KBS-official casting announcement could be directly confirmed, so this entry is marked unverified.
