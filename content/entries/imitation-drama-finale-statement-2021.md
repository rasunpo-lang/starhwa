Seonghwa, Yunho, San, and Jongho's finale statements for KBS2's "Imitation," via Sports Kyunghyang.

<!-- full-text -->

Following KBS2's "Imitation" concluding its run on July 23, 2021, Seonghwa, Yunho, San, and Jongho each released finale statements through their agency, reported by Sports Kyunghyang. Seonghwa, who played Sparkling's leader Se-young, reflects: "'이미테이션'을 만나 정말 행복했다. 드라마 속의 제 모습이 신기하면서 꿈만 같았다... 정말 멋지고 좋은 선배님들과 연기를 할 수 있어서 뜻깊은 경험이었다. 앞으로도 더 좋은 모습으로 찾아뵙는 성화가 되겠다" ("I was truly happy to have met 'Imitation.' Seeing myself in the drama felt strange, like a dream... it was a meaningful experience to act alongside such wonderful, talented senior actors. I'll keep coming back to you as a better Seonghwa"), thanking the director, crew, and viewers.

Seonghwa's Words — English Translation

This is a written statement released through the agency rather than a Q&A, so it is presented below as a single continuous translation rather than split into questions and answers.

"I was truly happy to have met 'Imitation.' Seeing myself in the drama felt strange, like a dream. I want to give my thanks to the director for giving me such a good opportunity, and to the production staff, who worked harder than anyone during filming. Through these people, I gained a lot of motivation to work even harder. It was also a meaningful experience to be able to act alongside such wonderful, great senior actors. I'll keep coming back to you all as an even better Seonghwa from now on. Thank you so much to all the viewers who watched 'Imitation,' and to our ATINY."

Translated directly from the original Korean text of the Sports Kyunghyang article.
