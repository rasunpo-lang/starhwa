Seonghwa starred in Isabel Marant's Spring-Summer 2025 Marant Homme menswear campaign alongside Kate Moss, shot in Paris by Robin Galiegue and styled by Emmanuelle Alt — his first individual fashion campaign, and the first time Isabel Marant has featured a K-pop artist in its men's line. Isabel Marant's own official campaign page (Journal) credits him directly: "the campaign features the iconic supermodel Kate Moss, and K-pop sensation Seonghwa from Ateez," listing him under "Models: Kate Moss, Seonghwa." The brand's own page does not use "ambassador" or "Global Ambassador" wording anywhere — that framing comes only from press coverage, so this entry describes his role as the source itself does: a campaign model, not a formally titled ambassador. The February 3, 2025 launch date is consistently reported across multiple outlets but is not itself dated on Isabel Marant's own page, so this entry remains unverified. ATEEZ's own official YouTube channel posted a video on this same campaign on October 23, 2024 — notably earlier than the February 2025 public launch, suggesting an early preview ahead of Isabel Marant's own rollout.
