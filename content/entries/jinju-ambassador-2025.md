Seonghwa appointed as Jinju City's public relations ambassador.

<!-- full-text -->

Seonghwa was appointed as an official public relations ambassador for Jinju, his hometown in South Gyeongsang Province, at a ceremony held December 19, 2025, selected for his local ties and global profile as an artist.

The ceremony took place at 1 p.m. at the Jinju Silk Museum, where he received his appointment plaque, read a commemorative letter as ambassador, and answered fan questions in a short Q&A.
