Seonghwa was appointed as an official public relations ambassador for Jinju, his hometown in South Gyeongsang Province, at a ceremony held December 19, 2025, selected for his local ties and global profile as an artist. The date and appointment are consistently corroborated across multiple sources, including what appears to be Jinju City's own YouTube upload of the ceremony, but that channel's ownership could not be independently confirmed, so this entry is marked unverified.
