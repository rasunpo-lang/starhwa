Teaser videos for the ATEEZ "MIIMCA x TELECA Collection Card," revealing photoshoots pairing Seonghwa with Hongjoong and with San, October 2026.

<!-- full-text -->

MIIMCA, the account behind the MIIM collection card line, announced an ATEEZ "MIIMCA x TELECA Collection Card" in October 2026. A first teaser on October 15 revealed the shoot with Seonghwa and Hongjoong, pointing to a release set for October 19 at midnight KST.

A second teaser on October 18, a day before release, previewed the shoot with San and Seonghwa and showed what the cards would look like.

The collection card went on sale on October 19, 2026, announced by MIIMCA with a release video pointing fans to the official retailers.

On October 23, 2026, Dispatch published an unboxing episode (Ep.3) of ATEEZ opening the collection card FAN BOX, which contains cards across nine grades.
