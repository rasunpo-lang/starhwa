ATEEZ win Hot Trend and Best Performance – Male and perform "UP," "Drip" and "BAD" on Day 1 of the Melon Music Awards 2026.

<!-- full-text -->

ATEEZ appeared on Day 1 of the Melon Music Awards 2026 (MMA2026) on November 14, 2026. Melon opened the day with a handwritten welcome message and a short invitation video, followed by the red carpet at 5 p.m. and the ceremony at 6 p.m., broadcast live on Melon and Wavve.

ATEEZ were announced among the Hot Trend winners, alongside RESCENE, and the Best Performance – Male winners, alongside RIIZE. On stage they performed "UP," the GOLDEN HOUR: Part.6 title track, together with "Drip" and "BAD"; Melon uploaded the full stage in 4K, two highlight Shorts and stage photos, and closed the night with a handwritten goodbye message for ATINY.
