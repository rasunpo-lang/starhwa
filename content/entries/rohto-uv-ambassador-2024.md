ATEEZ named PR ambassadors for Rohto Pharmaceutical's "Rohto UV" sunscreen line.

<!-- full-text -->

All eight ATEEZ members, Seonghwa included, were named PR ambassadors for Rohto Pharmaceutical's "Rohto UV" sunscreen line in Japan. A 15-second TV commercial, "UV Positive Revolution," began airing nationwide on March 25, 2024, alongside a web movie of the group dancing to their song "NOT OKAY" in lab coats at a mock sunscreen laboratory; the campaign later extended to advertising displays at Shibuya Station in June 2024.
