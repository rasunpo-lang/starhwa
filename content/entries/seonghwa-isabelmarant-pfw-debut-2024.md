Seonghwa's first Paris Fashion Week appearance, as a guest at Isabel Marant's SS25 show.

<!-- full-text -->

Seonghwa attended Isabel Marant's Spring/Summer 2025 show at Paris Fashion Week as a guest on September 29, 2024 — his first Paris Fashion Week appearance, ahead of his return the following March as a runway model. In a Hollywood Reporter interview published October 5, 2024, he reflected on the experience through the lens of ATEEZ's own group unity, described his personal approach to self-expression through clothing, and spoke about how the members support and inspire each other's individual style.
