Seonghwa attends the launch of SONGZIO's collaboration with EGON LAB.

<!-- full-text -->

Seonghwa appeared at the launch event for the SONGZIO x EGON LAB first collaboration collection on the afternoon of November 11, 2026, at a venue in Apgujeong, Seoul, in his role as a SONGZIO ambassador. The outfit he wore from the label drew the most attention. Dispatch filmed the photocall, and Korean outlets including Esquire Korea, Marie Claire Korea, Dazed Korea, WWD Korea, and L'OFFICIEL HOMMES Korea posted event coverage, many framed around Pepero Day and his interactions with attendees.
