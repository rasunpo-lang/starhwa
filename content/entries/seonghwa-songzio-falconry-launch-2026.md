Seonghwa attends the launch of SONGZIO x Heliot Emil's "Falconry" collaboration.

<!-- full-text -->

Seonghwa, in blonde hair, attends the launch event for "Falconry" — SONGZIO's second collaboration with Heliot Emil — at Gallery Noir Songzio, photographed by Korean digital fashion outlet Celeb Fashion Magazine, with further documentation posted by SONGZIO's own official account.

SONGZIO and Heliot Emil draw on falconry as the central idea for their collaboration, combining the discipline and precision of the practice with both brands' approach to functional design. The collection takes inspiration from falconry equipment and the structure of a bird's wing, reflected in layered silhouettes, reinforced details, technical fabrics, and streamlined shapes. The designs balance protection with lightness and structure with movement, creating garments that feel both sculptural and functional.
