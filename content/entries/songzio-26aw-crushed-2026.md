Official ATEEZ YouTube video of Seonghwa in SONGZIO's 26AW "Crushed, Cast, Constructed" collection.

<!-- full-text -->

Beyond the campaign film, SONGZIO's official Instagram shared "Moments" from the 26 Autumn Winter Collection "Crushed, Cast, Constructed" on October 3, 2026, as presented during Paris Fashion Week below the Pont Alexandre III. Seonghwa appears in the third slide of the carousel.

SONGZIO followed with two more carousels from the same presentation: a second "Moments" set on October 15, 2026, and a "Details" set on October 19, 2026, with Seonghwa in the seventh slide of the latter.

The collection revisits the turn of the 20th century, the early machine age, reworking the regimented frock coat, tailored three-piece, and high-collared shirt into a hyper-modern form.
