Seonghwa walks SONGZIO's 26SS "Polyptych" Paris runway show.

<!-- full-text -->

Official ATEEZ YouTube video of Seonghwa in SONGZIO's 26SS "Polyptych" collection. Vogue Korea's own report on the Paris runway show (held June 25, 2025 local time) describes Seonghwa wearing two looks: an intense, nature-inspired red look — layered-detail outerwear over a glossy skirt for an experimental silhouette, finished with ankle-strap sandals — followed by a black tweed set with metallic glitter accents, an oversized jacket paired with shorts. Vogue notes this followed his Isabel Marant runway debut that March, with a more relaxed presence this time.
