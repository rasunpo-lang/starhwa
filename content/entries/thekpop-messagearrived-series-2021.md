"[✉메시지가 도착했습니다]" is a series on THE K-POP, the official K-pop YouTube channel of SBS Medianet, with episodes uploaded in late August and September 2021. The channel's tags for EP.1, EP.6, and EP.7 list ATEEZ alongside PENTAGON, ONF, and THE BOYZ.

This entry gathers the series' episodes as a reference. The linked moments of EP.1 (from 8:17), EP.6 (from 9:00), and EP.7 (from 1:27) are the points flagged for Seonghwa; the two EP.5 videos are listed as part of the same series.
