The North America leg of ATEEZ's 2022 world tour "THE FELLOWSHIP: BREAK THE WALL," combining U.S. tour dates (November 7–28, 2022) and a single Canada date (December 2, 2022), per the official tour poster posted via ATEEZ's X account. The poster does not name individual cities or per-date stops; venue and ticketing announcements later confirmed stops in Oakland (added after the initial announcement), Anaheim, Glendale, Fort Worth, Chicago, Atlanta, Newark, and Hamilton, Ontario.
