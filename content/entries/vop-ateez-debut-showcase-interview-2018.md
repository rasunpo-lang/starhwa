ATEEZ's debut media showcase press Q&A for "TREASURE EP.1: All to Zero."

<!-- full-text -->

The official press Q&A held at ATEEZ's debut media showcase for "TREASURE EP.1: All to Zero," at Yes24 Live Hall on October 24, 2018, covered by Voice of the People. Seonghwa answers on his feelings the day of debut: "데뷔 당일이 되니 벅차고 감격스럽다... 연습실에서 우리만 보던 무대를 관객 앞에서 하게 되어 기대된다" ("Now that debut day is here, I feel overwhelmed and moved... I'm looking forward to performing in front of an audience the stage we used to only see in the practice room"), and explains the choice to release a double title track: "보여드리고 싶은 것이 많아 더블 타이틀곡을 정했다" ("We had so much we wanted to show, so we decided on a double title track"). Hongjoong and Wooyoung also answer on the group's debut concept and their "Block B junior group" introduction.

Seonghwa's Words — English Translation

On how debut day feels: "Now that it's actually debut day, I feel overwhelmed and moved. I'm looking forward to performing, in front of an audience, the stage that until now only we ourselves had seen in the practice room."

On why the group chose a double title track: "We had so much we wanted to show you, so we decided on a double title track."

Translated directly from the original Korean text of the Voice of the People article.
