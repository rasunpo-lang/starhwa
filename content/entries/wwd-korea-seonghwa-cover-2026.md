WWD Korea announced a solo cover story on Seonghwa for its September 2026 issue, timed to his upcoming 8th ATEEZ debut anniversary. Both official @wwdkorea Instagram posts were directly confirmed: "지금 가장 뜨거운 글로벌 아이콘... 에이티즈 성화" ("the hottest global icon right now... ATEEZ Seonghwa"), describing the shoot as capturing "his most honest and clearest face" away from the world tour spotlight, with credits for the full creative team. This is a separate publication and separate story from the January 2024 WWD.com (global) group anniversary interview already in the archive.
