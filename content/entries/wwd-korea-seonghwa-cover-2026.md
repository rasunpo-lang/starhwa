WWD Korea's solo Seonghwa cover story for its September 2026 issue.

WWD Korea announced a solo cover story on Seonghwa for its September 2026 issue, timed to his upcoming 8th ATEEZ debut anniversary. The official @wwdkorea Instagram account confirmed the story directly: "지금 가장 뜨거운 글로벌 아이콘... 에이티즈 성화" ("the hottest global icon right now... ATEEZ Seonghwa"), describing the shoot as capturing "his most honest and clearest face" away from the world tour spotlight, with credits for the full creative team. The rollout continued across several further @wwdkorea posts — the cover reveal itself (photo and video) on August 17, a behind-the-scenes reel on August 24, and a Q&A reel on September 3. This is a separate publication and separate story from the January 2024 WWD.com (global) group anniversary interview already in the archive.
